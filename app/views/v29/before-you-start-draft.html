Before you start

The Get help to buy food and milk (Healthy Start) service uses government data sources to check your eligibility.

If you qualify you'll get:

  • £4.65 each week of your pregnancy from the 10th week
  • £9.30 each week for children from birth to 1 year old
  • £4.65 each week for children between 1 and 4 years old

We'll ask for your:

  • name
  • address
  • date of birth
  • National Insurance number
  • baby’s due date (if you’re pregnant)
  • Universal Credit statement

The details you use to apply for Healthy Start must match the details on your Universal Credit claim.

Make sure that your details are up to date with DWP. You may wish to contact them to check or update your details before you apply.

If we need more information about your application, we might ask you to send us copies of documents that prove your:

  • identity (for example, birth certificate, marriage certificate, passport, driving licence or immigration visa)
  • eligibility (Universal Credit statement)
  • children's eligibility (birth certificate)

If we ask to see these, make sure you send copies of valid documents. We cannot accept expired documents.
